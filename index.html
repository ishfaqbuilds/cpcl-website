How it works

The CPCL – Eleanor OPEX solar model

Zero
capex

  1. Zero investment

    We finance the full system, from design to commissioning. Nothing comes off your balance sheet.

  2. Zero risk

    We own, operate and maintain the plant for the life of the agreement.

  3. Guaranteed savings

    You pay an agreed tariff for the solar power you use, fixed in the contract.

  4. Guaranteed performance

    Output is monitored continuously and backed by a contractual performance commitment.
