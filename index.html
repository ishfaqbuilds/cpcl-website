About CPCL

Safe, reliable renewable energy for Bangladesh.

Chittagong Power Company Limited (CPCL) is established to provide safe and reliable solutions and projects on renewable sources (solar, wind, geothermal) and is focused on enhancing efficiency to combat climate change. The core objective is to provide support to Bangladesh’s growing renewable energy agenda.

Currently, CPCL is focusing on OPEX-model rooftop solar solutions for industrial and commercial consumers. CPCL is a sister concern of BSRM Group, the market leader in Bangladesh’s steel industry, known for its strong, ethical business practices and its commitment to sustainable industrial growth. To provide the best services to its clients, BSRM Group aims to leverage its strong financial performance and national presence to provide its best support towards this Joint Venture with Eleanor Solar Solutions Limited.

CPCL’s initial focus: OPEX-model solar solutions

CPCL aims to deploy solar energy solutions across various industrial factory sheds and commercial building rooftops throughout Bangladesh, aligning with national renewable energy goals and supporting the decarbonization of manufacturing operations.

How it works

The CPCL – Eleanor OPEX solar model

  • Zero investment
  • Zero risk
  • Guaranteed savings
  • Guaranteed performance

Under the OPEX Model, CPCL finances, owns, operates, and maintains the solar power plant installed on your rooftop or premises.

What this means for you

  1. Zero CAPEX

    No upfront investment.

  2. Lower electricity cost

    Solar tariff lower than grid price.

  3. Guaranteed performance

    Through Eleanor’s technical capabilities.

  4. No operational burden

    CPCL & Eleanor handle everything.

  5. Carbon reduction

    Direct contribution to the green energy transition.
